### ARC, the Autonomous Recovery Controller

Real-time fault tolerance for neural network training. ARC watches your run, predicts training failures and recovers from them automatically, so a bad step doesn't cost you the whole job.

**[Get started →](https://pyarc.pages.dev)** &nbsp;&nbsp; [Source](https://github.com/ClearBound-Labs/ARC) &nbsp;·&nbsp; AGPL-3.0

### Research

We publish what we learn about how models fail:

- **Scale-Dependent Structural Mimicry in Distilled Reasoning Models.** Do distilled models reason or imitate the shape of reasoning?
- **How Neural Collapse Diverges in Self-Supervised and Supervised Learning.** Representation geometry, frame by frame.
- **Lagrange Paralysis.** Decoding deadlocks, contractive geometry and the price of escaping them.
- **Trained to Agree.** How post-training homogenizes LLM committees and what committee rewards can and cannot fix.
